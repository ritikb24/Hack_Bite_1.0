Clear ground rules.

A few simple rules keep the challenge fair, collaborative, and focused on what matters: your ideas and how you bring them to life.

01 /

Build as a team

Teams must have 2–4 registered members. Every participant should contribute meaningfully to the final solution.

02 /

Respect the two-stage format

STAGE 01 Internet and AI tools are not allowed. STAGE 02 Internet and AI tools may be used as announced by the organisers.

03 /

Keep it original

Submit work created by your team during the challenge. Clearly acknowledge any third-party assets or code you use when permitted.

04 /

Be ready to present

Prepare a short demo and explain the problem, your approach, and the impact of your solution. Follow the time limits shared at check-in.

05 /

Respect the space

Follow mentor and organiser instructions, treat other teams respectfully, and keep your work area safe and tidy.
